    About the DURF Metadata Monitor

    DURF Theme 2: Metadata Quality Improvement — proof of concept with mock data

    This page sketches a monitor for DURF Theme 2 – Metadata Quality Improvement (see the DURF Gantt chart), as a companion to the DURF Full Text Monitor. Its purpose is to give evidence for how incomplete the metadata in Dutch CRIS systems is: how many records carry a DOI, an ORCID, a ROR, an abstract, a grant ID, an open access status and a corresponding author — and, when a field is filled in, whether its value is actually well formed.

    Mock data: all numbers, charts and example errors are generated at random in your browser. They only illustrate layout and possible charts. The Plan & feasibility tab describes what can really be measured.

    Three states per field

    • Valid — filled in and passes the check (format, check digit, controlled vocabulary).
    • Invalid — filled in, but fails the check (e.g. a DOI stored as a URL, an ORCID with a wrong check digit).
    • Missing — empty or absent.

    Planned source: SURF ORI DuckLake, schema cris, harvested via OAI-PMH (CERIF-XML, OpenAIRE profile) from Dutch institutional repositories and CRIS systems.
